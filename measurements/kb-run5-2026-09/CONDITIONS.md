## What changed since round four, and why

**1. The ground is chosen for coverage, and that is a dropped condition.** Round four's blind pick
put the run on `/api/pricing`, where the register held nothing, so the licence to act on a
`confirmed` entry unverified was exercised zero times — the treatment's second half went untested.
`pick-covered-namespace.mjs` returns `/api/order` with 7 licensed entries. Three conditions — a
corpus-blind pick, covered ground, questions the author did not write — are satisfiable two at a
time. This round drops the first and says so on the task page.

**2. The questions were frozen before the namespace was known.** `QUESTION-TEMPLATE.md`, committed
in `f0386d0`; the picker in `841c5da`. That ordering is checkable in the history and it is what stops
the questions being shaped to what the register holds, which is what happened, smaller, in round
four.

**3. There is a control arm.** Arm A runs the same task with no register. Two things come out of it:
a capability comparison the previous rounds could not make on one task, and tier-two verification —
where arm A independently establishes one of the 7, its report becomes a `from:` row on that entry,
which is an attested confirmation by a party that never read the register.

**4. The register now says what it means.** Two corrections landed since round four's brief:
`confirmations` counted evidence rows and was overstated for eight entries (it counts independent
parties now), and a new `attested` column says whether any party wrote down what they saw — **5 of
22** licensed entries do. The brief explains both, including the admission that the old column was
wrong.

**5. The brief warns that the pin is stale.** `derived/pin.json` says `3.1007.26`; the stand served
`.27` during round four and nobody has re-extracted. Arm C is told to report the version it observes
and not to try to correct the stamp.

**6. `confirm` now requires `--note`.** An agreement that describes nothing no longer writes. This
changes what both arms can do and is deliberately the same for both.

## The arrival hook is still off

One treatment per run. Its three filters remain unbuilt.

## Order of the two arms

**Arm A first, then arm C.** Arm A is the one that must not be contaminated, and the operator
running both is a channel: having just watched arm C cite `KB-4CCC2DD6`, it is hard to stay silent
during arm A. Run the control while nobody knows what the treated arm did.

Both are fresh sessions that have never seen this directory.

## What this run can show, and what it cannot

**Can:** whether an agent handed a licence to act without re-verifying actually takes it, on ground
where the licence applies; and whether the entries it acts on hold.

**Cannot:** anything about rediscovery. The ground was picked because the register covers it, so arm
A re-establishing one of these 7 is commissioned by the ground. That number is useful as tier-two
verification and worthless as evidence about the register.
