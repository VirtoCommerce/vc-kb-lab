# Erratum — corrections to the architecture brief, found by reproducing it

**Send this to the reviewer if they already have `REVIEW-BRIEF-ARCHITECTURE.md`.** The brief was
handed over, then re-derived from its own scripts a few hours later, the way the prompt asks the
reviewer to. Four things did not reproduce. All four are corrected in the brief as of commit below;
this page exists so the reviewer is not the one who has to find them.

Two of the four moved in the direction that flatters the project. That is why they are itemised
rather than quietly patched.

---

## 1. The live plane is 73% used, not 69% or 70%

`measurements/kb-utilization-2026-09/`.

The brief's table said `captured — 78 entries — 69% used`, and its prose said `81 … 70%`. The script
said **89 / 67%**, because it counted the 11 **retired** entries — superseded entries that are
unused by design — in with the live ones. Three numbers for one row, none of them right.

The script now splits them. The corrected rows:

| shape | total | used |
|---|---|---|
| captured (active) | 78 | **73%** |
| captured (retired, unused by design) | 11 | 27% |

**The headline is unaffected**: the dead share of the whole corpus is 78.0%, and 78.1% excluding
retired entries. The correction makes the agent-written plane look *better* than the brief claimed.

## 2. The arrival denominator is 4,013 calls, not 4,038

`measurements/kb-arrival-2026-09/`. Twenty-two logs, **4,013** calls, **310** of them `kb-calls`.
The README stated 4,038 in three places and 311 in one; the brief repeated 4,038 twice. The figure
predates the same day's rewrite of `replay-logs.mjs`, which changed how calls are counted, and
nobody re-derived the page.

**Everything computed from it is unchanged**: 316 arrivals, 246 on entries that existed before their
run, 3 of 9 source subjects, and the +5 / +6 deltas against the two arms with no base all reproduce
exactly.

## 3. The measurements inventory miscounted itself

Ten directories, not nine, and the claim that each carries "a README plus a runnable script" was
false for three of them. Eight now carry a README; `kb-comparison` and `kb-live` carry dated result
pages instead, which the line now says.

## 4. The source door is listed as a fix that landed. Its own script scores it at zero

**This is the one that matters.** `measurements/kb-sourcedoor-2026-09/` had no README, and the brief
listed the source door in the "what changed" table with no number beside it. Run the script and it
reports that across all fifteen questions on record the door fired **zero** times.

Reproducing it changed our reading of it twice:

* First reading — *the door is broken*. Wrong.
* The script was **miscounting**: it scored a procedural redirect as a MISS. Both "misses" are
  questions like `create a promotion with a coupon code`, which `ask` now correctly hands to a flow
  that answers them exactly (`KB-EB228603`, `KB-A54C919F`). Fixed; the run now separates `redirect`
  from `MISS`.
* Corrected reading — **the door has had zero occasions, not zero successes.** Thirteen of fifteen
  questions are answered outright, and measurement 2 shows six of those answers are adjacent rather
  than right. *A mechanism that fires only on a MISS cannot be exercised by a base that has stopped
  missing.* The drift and the silent door are one finding.

The brief now carries this as measurement 4, and says plainly that the door is **unmeasured and
unmeasurable by replay** — only a live run asking something the base genuinely cannot answer will
settle it.
